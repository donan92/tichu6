# Court illustration prompts

Built-in OpenAI image generation, 2026-11-23. Transparent original PNGs are in `source/`; `npm run assets:cards` produces the shipped WebP images. The UI suit ink is rose-red `#c92e4b` and blue-black `#172535`.

### jack

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original jack court playing-card illustration, traditional French/English poker deck woodcut line art, recognizable human royal character, not an abstract emblem. Young clean-shaven male jack with feathered cap holding a halberd. Double-ended rotationally symmetric bust portrait, upright upper half and upside-down lower half meeting seamlessly at waist. Tall narrow 2:3 artwork. Crisp blue-black ink outlines, ivory face, rose pink and muted gold garments. Flat print colors. Fill the artwork with the figure, readable on a small mobile card. Transparent background. No card border, no letters, no numbers, no suit symbols, no words or watermark.
