# Original game audio

These are fixed audio assets, not generated on the player's device.

- `lobby.mp3`: approved straight-time jazz-synth lobby theme, 96 BPM, 60 seconds.
- `game.mp3`: mellow game theme, 88 BPM, approximately 65 seconds.
- Remaining files: sixteen short event cues, including the revised dragon/shuffle.

Provenance: original project compositions from the approved preview, rendered
once from commit1562078 on the development machine. They retain that preview's
instrument character; they are not new studio recordings or stock music.
The source PCM contained no clipped samples. Converted with FFmpeg to44.1kHz MP3,
160kbps stereo music and96kbps mono effects, at85% source level with8ms/12ms edge
fades. Vite emits content-hashed URLs for cache-safe releases. Total2.91MB.

No external service, samples, voices, artist imitation or paid licenses used.
Physical iPhone playback and subjective listening quality remain unverified.
